My Story

Hello! I'm Alex Johnson, a passionate web developer and UI/UX designer with over 5 years of experience creating digital solutions that make a difference.

My journey began with a Computer Science degree, but I quickly discovered my love for the creative side of technology. I started freelancing while still in college, and since then, I've had the privilege of working with businesses of all sizes, from startups to established corporations.

What drives me is the opportunity to solve real-world problems through code and design. I believe that great websites should not only look beautiful but also provide exceptional user experiences and deliver tangible results for businesses.

When I'm not coding, you can find me exploring new technologies, contributing to open-source projects, or hiking in the mountains to clear my mind and find inspiration.
